This is where I keep smaller observations that do not need a full project writeup yet. They are closer to working notes than finished answers.

<div class="lab-note-list">
  <article class="lab-note">
    <span>movement_systems / ongoing</span>
    <h2>The transition is part of the state</h2>
    <p>A movement state can look correct by itself and still feel wrong when the player enters or leaves it. I try to treat transitions as part of the behavior rather than as cleanup between two finished animations.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="lab-note">
    <span>systems_thinking / observation</span>
    <h2>Edge cases expose the real design</h2>
    <p>The ideal path tells me whether the basic idea works. Interruptions, direction changes, combat states, and moving surfaces tell me whether the system itself is coherent.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="lab-note">
    <span>learning_by_doing / method</span>
    <h2>Start with a problem I can picture</h2>
    <p>When I am learning a new AI tool, I get further by attaching it to a practical side project. The problem gives me a way to judge the output, instead of learning features without knowing what they are for.</p>
  </article>
</div>
